# Word list

`words.txt.gz` is derived from the SCOWL-based hunspell `en_US` dictionary, expanded from
its stems and affix rules into ~113,000 inflected forms. Proper nouns are excluded.

SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) is maintained by Kevin Atkinson.
